La Ceibona weather station · IESPAR102 · Mojón de Esparza, Costa Rica

By day La Ceibona is warmer than downtown Esparza; by night it is cooler than the coast

We compared our temperature with two nearby stations, 5 minutes at a time, for the 2026 rainy season. Against Esparza we are warmer for about half the day and hardly ever colder. Against the seaside station we are colder for almost three quarters of the day, all night long, and only catch up in the late morning.

    How much of the day we are hotter or colder

    Each 5 minutes counts as hotter or colder when our reading differs from the neighbour's by more than °C; smaller gaps are within what the sensors can resolve, so they count as about the same.

            Why only 2026: the Esparza station moved

            IESPAR72 used to stand about 3 km from us and moved to downtown Esparza during the 2026 dry season. The daytime gap shows it: a median of +2.6 °C a week through June to September 2025 and +1.7 °C in 2026, with no step within either season (nights went from +0.1 to +0.4 °C). Its Weather Underground location was never updated, so the old site's data looks like the new one's. Mixing the two years would describe neither site, so everything above uses 2026 only.

              Limits of this comparison

              • Rainy season only (June to September). The dry season, with clearer skies and drier air, may look quite different.
              • The seaside station only reports from 30 July 2026: days.
              • The three stations have different sensors, radiation shields and mountings. Part of our midday lead may come from the instruments rather than the climate; a gap that holds night and day, sunny and cloudy, is more trustworthy than its exact size.
              • The neighbours' readings come from other owners' Weather Underground stations. Their terms allow personal use only, with no derived data published, so this report is for our own review and stays off the dashboard.
